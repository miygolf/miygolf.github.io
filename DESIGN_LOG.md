# Design log — one entry per video

Read this before designing a new video: the next one must NOT reuse the look below.

## 2026-11-06 · putting-wrist (Masalah putting golfer baru: guna wrist)

- Project: `putting-wrist/` (HyperFrames 0.9.18), 1080×1920, 30 fps, 42.0 s
- Look name: **"Yardage Book"**
  - Palette: fairway green `#0E3B2E`, paper cream `#F4EDE0`, alignment-stick yellow `#FFC21A`, flag red `#E5392B`
  - Type: League Gothic (titles), Space Mono (labels/tags), Archivo Black (captions)
  - Shapes: cream paper cards with 5 px green border + hard 14 px offset shadow; full-width green chapter band
    with yellow rules; two parallel yellow "alignment stick" bars as the recurring motif; contour-line texture
  - Captions: Archivo Black uppercase, cream fill + thick green stroke, word-by-word karaoke, key words gold, "wrist/salah" red
  - Graphics placement: sky band (y 176–540) on wide shots, right column (x 712–1040) on close-ups
  - Transitions: hard-cut punch-ins (1.0 ↔ 1.1/1.2), band wipes, blur swaps; end card = footage shrinks into a framed card
- Music: Infraction "Sax Beat" (funk retro, 112 BPM), ducked to 0.15 under voice
- SFX: Pixabay set bundled with HyperFrames (whoosh variants, pop, click-soft, ping, sparkle, impact-bass, error, cinematic whoosh)

**Avoid next time:** cream-card + hard-shadow style, League Gothic, green/cream/yellow palette, stroked karaoke captions,
contour texture, yellow double-bar motif, footage-shrinks-to-card ending.
