## Lessons

- TikTok Shop down-ranks shoppable videos that are only product B-roll + text overlay +
  music ("non-interactive"). Keep a person interacting with the product on screen, keep
  footage full-frame (not shrunk into cards/slideshows), and prefer a real voiceover.
- Avoid health/benefit claims ("kurangkan tekanan") and pressure lines ("selagi ada stock").
- The owner wants a video, not a slideshow: long continuous takes, straight cuts, footage always
  full frame with nothing behind it. Points and SFX are fine; no background music and no
  notification-style "ting" sounds.
- Message focus: who uses it and what it does well, stated only from what the footage shows.
