## Changes from the original brief

1. **Lighting conflict.** The scene calls for dim, dramatic light, but the generic rules asked for natural studio or ambient lighting. I kept dim and dramatic for this scene and kept the "accurate shadows" requirement.
2. **Voice tone conflict.** The script is angry and suspicious, but the VO rule asked for friendly and relaxed delivery. I resolved this as *playful-dramatic suspicion* (skit-style), so it still sounds santai and authentic.
3. **Product visibility.** A face-only close-up would hide the bag, so the camera drifts to bring the bag and its logo into frame while it stays one continuous shot.
4. **Script.** Kept word-for-word.
