# Review — "Jadual Sarapan" (preview stage)

## Creative calls
- Look: bus-station departure board + bus ticket (the shop is at Stesen Bas Damansara Damai), in the
  62 Breakfast Club egg-yolk yellow with Fredoka (the menu's rounded voice) and Space Mono split-flaps.
  Deliberately unlike the earlier edits (golf before/after, "Kes Ditutup" case file, highway signage).
- Hook: scarcity. Frame 0 = the plate + "Sarapan ni… BUKA 3 JAM JE." on the board; the hours flip in,
  then "Lambat sikit, melepas." Location is the second beat, price the third.
- Food is never under a word: S1 pushes the tray footage 200 px down below the board (food starts ≥ y 517,
  board ends y 488); S2 (the plate photo, three framings) carries no text at all; text over the shop and
  the menu only; the end card is on a yolk world, not over food.
- Safe zone: every word in y 188–1300 and x 60–940 (TikTok/IG top bar, bottom caption/handle area,
  right action rail).
- One riser→impact: the "BUKA SEL – SAB" stamp on the ticket.
- Cuts sit on the 121 BPM grid; the music drop lands on the cut to the plate (3.0 s).
