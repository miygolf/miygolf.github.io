Overswing. You seronok tengok John Daly buat macam tu, tapi berapa puluh tahun dia practice.
So you tak boleh ikut, sebab maybe body and stability tak sama.
Again, overswing. Macam mana? Daripada tepi, angkat… cukup.
Tak perlu nak melampau-lampau belakang ni, sebab penatlah.
Bila panjang-panjang swing ni, dahlah you lambat nak datang.
Bola pun tak kena, weight shift tak kena, transfer tak betul, finishing lagilah.
Again, make sure you ada that control of the backswing.
Parallel to the ground kalau boleh, letak sini pun okay.
And you ada that control, finish dengan lebih baik.
