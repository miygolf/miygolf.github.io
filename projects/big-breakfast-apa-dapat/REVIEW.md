# Review — "Apa Dapat?" Big Breakfast breakdown (preview stage)

## Creative calls
- Built to the reference's grammar on request ("buat macam video ni"): same beats, same copy where it
  fits, yellow sticker chips, Archivo Black headlines, numbered dark labels with a dot and leader.
- Six items instead of four: the plate in our footage also carries toast + butter and baked beans.
- Headlines never sit on food: hook and closing shot push the tray 250 px down under a fade, so the
  words sit on the steel table; on the item tour only the small numbered label touches the item, as in
  the reference. All words in y 200–1310, x 60–940.
- Added what the reference lacked: location "Stesen Bas Damansara Damai" and hours "Sel – Sab · 8 – 11 pagi".
- Cuts on the 100 BPM bar grid (2.4 s); each item is two beats (1.2 s).
